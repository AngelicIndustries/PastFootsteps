PastFootsteps equipment accent provenance
=========================================

Rain accent
-----------
The event equipment.rain uses the already bundled Presence Footsteps
water_stereofix/water_through recordings. They are byte-identical in the
audited AET and Dynamic Surroundings copies, and originate from Presence
Footsteps commit bed73687a68ce5fd02bb1a290fef8cbdf6980ceb. The PF MIT notice
in ../presencefootsteps/LICENSE-MIT.txt applies. Credit: Hurricaaane / Presence Footsteps.

Armor recordings
----------------
The armor/*.ogg recordings were transferred from the verified Dynamic
Surroundings 3.6.2.0 source pin
091248a8f88c2dc1c4a9f5fd2818072dcea49d64, via the byte-identical AET set.
Dynamic Surroundings is MIT, copyright 2018 OreCruncher, but its CREDITS.md
contains recording-specific notices that remain applicable here:

* light_1..3: Samulis (now sgossner), Freesound Foley pack, CC BY 4.0.
  https://freesound.org/people/sgossner/packs/18201/
* medium_walk1..3: the same Samulis Foley pack, CC BY 4.0, plus "chain drop"
  by satanicupsman, CC0.
* heavy_walk*, heavy_run*: "Clanking Armor" by LordSane, attribution required.
  Dynamic Surroundings refers to Nexus mod 24352 and its permissions. The
  original permission page is unavailable, so this attribution is retained
  without claiming blanket MIT coverage for those recordings.
* heavy_foot*, crystal_*, and slime_*: the audited DS sounds.json and
  CREDITS.md contain no asset-specific contrary notice. They remain under the
  Dynamic Surroundings repository MIT license.
