Presence Footsteps resource provenance
======================================

Acoustic definitions, biome variance data, variator data, sounds.json, and
audio files were copied from Sollace/Presence-Footsteps tag
1.12.3+1.21.11, commit d0d860591287656244e7236a9131004d1d0ddee6:

https://github.com/Sollace/Presence-Footsteps/tree/d0d860591287656244e7236a9131004d1d0ddee6

The Minecraft 1.7 registry block map and primitive fallback map were copied
from the same repository at commit
08cc8a0b3d1225f6b781e8a0aa5e79ed1c90f927:

https://github.com/Sollace/Presence-Footsteps/tree/08cc8a0b3d1225f6b781e8a0aa5e79ed1c90f927

Both pinned source revisions contain the MIT license reproduced in
LICENSE-MIT.txt. No resources from the project's later PolyForm Shield era
are included here.

The original resource namespace remains "presencefootsteps" and the bundled
sound event names retain their "pf_presence" prefix for compatibility with
Presence Footsteps sound packs. The imported mono assets use the separate
"presencefootstepsmono" namespace.

PastFootsteps adaptations: sound events use the 1.7.10 player category.
PF-emitted samples override that resource default at playback using the
source actor's player/neutral/hostile category, including mixer updates
and cleanup. Shared registry entries and non-PF sounds are unchanged.
The Forge movement, material and acoustic adapters implement the MIT-era
PF substrate and acoustic models using Minecraft 1.7.10 APIs. Modern vanilla
sound names without 1.7 equivalents are mapped to similar bundled sounds.

The additional presencefootstepsmono sound namespace was imported from
Sollace/Presence-Footsteps commit
c009d4a9fd10e9c3fa8bc75238bf26267f17ca1c:

https://github.com/Sollace/Presence-Footsteps/tree/c009d4a9fd10e9c3fa8bc75238bf26267f17ca1c

The swimming acoustic selection, movement-controlled volume ranges, and
remote swim scaling are also adapted from TerrestrialStepSoundGenerator
and VaryingAcoustic at this MIT-licensed revision. PastFootsteps supplies
timing through its shared contact engine and retains shallow-water wading.

Takeoff/landing event selection, the standing-jump and hard-landing
thresholds, and grouped material deduplication also follow this revision's
TerrestrialStepSoundGenerator. Physical contact groups remain owned by
PastFootsteps, with separate sound selection and scoped native fall fallback.

Source-class volume, the running adjustment and relative-size pitch formula
follow SoundEngine, MotionTracker and sound/player/ImmediateSoundPlayer at
the same MIT revision. Running adjustment defaults to zero. Its squared-speed
ramp is adapted to PF's shared displacement tracker and profile runSpeed,
with smoothing for 1.7 remote interpolation. Positive adjustments reserve
headroom by normalizing walking gain, preserving running contrast below
Minecraft's volume ceiling. Profiles provide the nominal
adult width missing from 1.7's entity-type API. Child gait timing is unchanged.

Per-entity block-map precedence and special-value handling are adapted from
Isolator, Lookup, SoundsKey and AssociationPool at the same MIT revision.
PastFootsteps selects shared named maps through inherited entity profiles;
its map paths and metadata selectors are specific to the Forge 1.7 adapter.

Foliage contact, messy-entry/swimming selection and footwear/sprint rules
are adapted from PFSolver and TerrestrialStepSoundGenerator at the same MIT
revision. The adapter uses its shared contacts and per-actor acoustic-name
deduplication with a two-tick entry cooldown instead of a second wall clock.

Acoustic range positions follow VaryingAcoustic and Range.on in both audited
MIT branches, including finite extrapolation (used by winged landing volume).
The Forge adapter bounds invalid audio results; swimming clamps its own
intensity independently. No built-in MIT generator requests gliding_pitch.

Acoustic delay merging, early/late tolerances and distance arrival follow
DelayedAcoustic, Options, DelayedSoundPlayer and ImmediateSoundPlayer at the
same MIT revision. The Forge adapter uses bounded monotonic queues, client
listener coordinates, one tick of minimum late grace, and actor lifetime
checks. It fixes the upstream ranged-delay random-bound bug. The skippable
presence option is also exposed as an adapter JSON field; vanilla paused
menus clear pending audio. Distance arrival has a separate stale deadline.

Giant and magma cube locomotion coverage follows the BIPED entries in
config/locomotionmap.json in both audited MIT branches. Forge uses the
legacy IDs Giant and LavaSlime with the existing shared contact tracker.
Profile volume/stride tuning and 1.7 nominal widths are adapter data;
no additional sound assets or specialized hopping generator were copied.

Wildcard/group lookup follows the purpose of MIT StateLookup's global
wildcard and registry tags. Registry-name star patterns and #ore: selectors
are Forge-specific extensions, using 1.7 metadata and Ore Dictionary
membership. Existing PF exact-key and entity-map priority remain intact;
modern block-state predicates and tag names still require conversion.

Derived/base and appearance lookup follows AssociationPool, DerivedBlock,
MStairsBlock and PFSolver at the audited MIT revisions. The Forge adapter
uses 1.7 stair constructor fields, bounded static base chains and a public
top-face provider API, preserving physical collision geometry. Supplementary
substrates do not inherit ordinary walking sounds. Carpenter's Blocks
support calls its public API by reflection; no Carpenter's source or assets
are copied. The MIT name-to-leaves heuristic is not included.

The 455 OGG files imported from that namespace are byte-identical MIT-source
assets and are all mono. Its imported sound event categories were changed
from "master" to the existing 1.7.10 "player" category. Local aliases cover
rain, armor, crumbly stone, fire, and waxed surfaces. Existing mono armor
recordings are referenced in the original namespace without copying them.

Three additional light_1..3 armor files are mono downmixes of the existing
stereo recordings, encoded with FFmpeg/libvorbis at quality 5. These modified
copies retain the original Samulis / sgossner attribution and CC BY 4.0
terms described in ../pastfootsteps/EQUIPMENT-SOURCE-NOTICE.txt. They are not
covered by the PF MIT asset license. Original stereo recordings are unchanged.

Thin overlay selection follows MIT PFSolver's mapped carpet substrate and
sub-0.3-block shape check. The 1.7 adapter uses bounded state bounds for
non-colliding floor coverings, guards unloaded reads, restores shared block
bounds and intersects decal geometry with the physical support.

Foliage contact-region handling extends the audited MIT footwear strategy:
body vegetation above the boot region shares the existing gait and acoustic
claims without being suppressed by boots. Bounds-based body intersection
and 1.7 double-plant upper/lower metadata resolution are Forge adapter work.
Fern, tall grass and rose bush mappings follow both audited MIT block maps.

The leaves and softleaves acoustics add a body-only rustle event referencing
the existing upstream leaf rustle at its original volume/pitch. This local
adaptation excludes their dirt layer for body brushing without changing the
upstream ground/climbing event definitions or any recordings.

Registry mapping diagnostics adapt the audited MIT BlockReport/StateLookup
report concept to Forge 1.7 metadata and the local effective material resolver.
The command, provenance fields, missing-definition checks and JSON layout are
local adapter work. Reports do not evaluate world-dependent appearances.

Fast horse stride extension follows the intent of TerrestrialStepSoundGenerator's
horse speed correction in both audited MIT revisions. That code tests squared
horizontal speed above 0.1 and triples its distance threshold. The local adapter
uses a smooth configurable stride curve above sqrt(0.1), reaching 1.5x at 0.6
blocks/tick, to fit its existing four-foot diagonal trot rather than copying the
upstream modifier's three-event timing or its absolute multiplier. Other gaits
retain their existing timing unless explicitly configured.
