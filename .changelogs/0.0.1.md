* First release: Presence Footsteps on Forge 1.7.10, with its sounds
* Footsteps by block material for walking, running, climbing, jumping and landing, with soft, running and hard landings, wading, and swimming in water and lava
* Other players and mobs make footsteps too: paws, hooves, chickens, horses, slimes and magma cubes, and spiders on walls. Creatures without a profile keep their own sounds
* Your own steps play in stereo; other players' and mobs' steps come from where they walk and arrive later from further away
* Foliage rustles as you walk through it, armor clinks and rain splashes with your steps
* Footprints, which fade with the weather, with configurable surfaces and styles
* Stairs, carpets, pressure plates, snow layers, Carpenter's Blocks covers and Et Futurum Requiem boats sound like what they're made of
* Even Mo' Bends' jointed spiders place their feet where their legs are
* Takes over Dynamic Surroundings' footsteps while enabled
* Works on any server. A server with Past Footsteps sends other players' and mobs' exact movement, for better timed steps
* Volume settings for your own steps, other players, mobs, foliage, armor and swimming, a key to toggle footsteps and a material display, in the mod's config screen
* Resource packs can change materials, sounds and entity profiles; `/pf acoustics` exports the current sounds as a resource pack to edit
* Mods can add surfaces and silence a mob's footsteps through the API
* An Extra Touch's armor and rain footsteps and its footprints moved here
* Translations from Presence Footsteps in 12 more languages
